Copper Room / Apps / Copperlight

Copperlight v1.0

Live visuals that move with your music. Kick, snare, hi-hat: each one drives the picture. Runs offline from a single file. 100% free.

Launch in browser Download · ZIP · 402 KB Manual

Tested in Opera Air and Chrome; built for Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Opera). Safari on a Mac is not supported; on iPad, Safari works without MIDI and System audio. Firefox untested.

01 What it does

Built for live sets

I made Copperlight for my own shows: it listens to the music and turns every hit into picture.

  • 01

    Hears your sound

    Separate detectors for kick, snare and hi-hat. Feed it a mic, line input, system audio or audio files.

  • 02

    Ten modes

    Video synth, ink, strobe blocks, Voronoi, two scopes, tunnel, moiré and two typography modes.

  • 03

    Nine stages, nine lenses

    Stack feedback, glitch, ASCII, scanlines and more. Then bend it with lens, symmetry, tiles, twist and spin.

  • 04

    Play it live

    128 preset slots, A/B looks, dice, 8 macros and up to 8 LFOs. MIDI learn with clock sync.

  • 05

    Record

    Capture picture and audio to a 1080p or 4K video file.

  • 06

    Fully offline

    No account, no install, no network calls. Everything runs locally in your browser.
