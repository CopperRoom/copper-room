Copperlightv1.0

By Copper Room

Live visuals that move with your music. Kick, snare, hi-hat: each one drives the picture. Runs offline from a single file. 100% Free.

Launch in browserDownload · HTML · 490 KBManual

Tested in Opera Air and Chrome. Other browsers not tested.

What it does
01

Hears your sound

Separate detectors for kick, snare and hi-hat. Feed it a mic, line input, system audio or audio files.

02

Ten modes

Video synth, ink, strobe blocks, Voronoi, two scopes, tunnel, moiré, two typography modes.

03

Eight stages, nine lenses

Stack feedback, glitch, ASCII, scanlines and more. Then bend it with lens, symmetry, tiles, twist, spin.

04

Play it live

128 preset slots, A/B looks, dice, 8 macros, up to 8 LFOs. MIDI learn with clock sync.

05

Record

Capture picture and audio to a 1080p or 4K video file.

06

Fully offline

No account, no install, no network calls. Everything runs locally in your browser.
