05 About

No presets
No templates
Just the sound

Portrait of João at the desk

I'm João Bonifácio, an electronic musician, producer and engineer based in Porto and working between Europe and Los Angeles. I started out touring as a roadie, then ran a commercial recording and mixing studio for ten years.

I work by improvising. Where others reach for a preset, I reach for the instrument, so every record carries the texture of how it was made.

  • Releases
    Hanami (2020) and Lockdown Tapes (2022)
  • Studied
    Masterclass with Brian Eno
  • Played
    Paredes de Coura festival, and three full shows with Digitópia at Casa da Música
  • Teaches
    Synthesis workshops, from the basics to modular systems, and talks on production and live setups
